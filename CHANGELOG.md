# Changelog

What changed from one release to the next, for the people who use the app. The
version is under the logo on the sign-in screen and at the foot of your
account menu; the details of any change are in `NEURALBRAIN.md`.

## 1.0 Caesious

The first version of Trainability Coach, live at trainabilitycoach.com.

- **Sign in with your Studio account.** The same email and password as
  Trainability Studio; there is no separate Coach account. A forgotten
  password is reset from the sign-in screen, and the temporary one works in
  both apps. Each app keeps its own sign-in, so signing in or out of one
  leaves the other as it was.
- **The home screen.** Today's date and a greeting by name, and the four parts
  of Coach: Today, Clients, Programs and Messages. Clients is open, with how
  many clients you have; the other three are marked Coming soon.
- **Your clients.** The Clients tab shows everyone on your own list in
  Studio, read from Studio each time so it is never out of date: each one's
  last session, and tapped open, their email, phone, birthday, the day they
  became your client and their next booked session. Search by name, email or
  phone, and archived clients kept under their own tab. Clients are still
  added and changed in Studio.
- **Studio's header and tab bar.** The Coach logo and name across the top,
  and a floating bar at the bottom for Home, Clients, Programs and Messages.
- **Switch between Coach and Studio.** Tap the Coach logo and a panel folds
  down with both apps; Studio is one tap away.
- **Your account menu.** Your photo in the top corner opens a menu with your
  name, email and kind of account, Open Studio, Log out and the version.
- **Billy.** Studio's assistant, beside the badges in the header: ask him
  about the clients on your list, or send him a photo or a PDF — a food log,
  a form, a program — and he reads it. He changes nothing. Shown only where
  the server has a key for him.
- **My equipment.** In your account menu: a short checklist of what you have
  to train with, and the particular pieces a program has asked about. Billy
  checks it before suggesting exercises, asks about a piece once, and notes
  your answer there for you to change.
- **My exercises,** in the account menu: your own names for exercises, tied
  to Coach's library, names still to sort out, and exercises of your own,
  which you can suggest for everybody's library. Billy uses your names, and
  notes one when you tell him.
- **Billy suggests from the library, already checked.** Ask him for
  something instead of an exercise and he offers only what fits the
  equipment at the place, the client's level and their limitations.
- **Review Coach IQ,** for the Primary Master: the library one family at a
  time and the conditions table, each to Approve or Flag with a comment, with
  a count of how far through; trainers' suggestions for the library; and
  Download reviews, to write the marks into the library itself.
- **Light or dark,** following your phone or computer.
