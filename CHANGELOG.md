# Changelog

What changed from one release to the next, for the people who use the app. The
version is under the logo on the sign-in screen and at the foot of your
account menu; the details of any change are in `NEURALBRAIN.md`.

## 1.0 Caesious

The first version of Trainability Coach, live at trainabilitycoach.com.

- **Sign in with your Studio account.** The same email and password as
  Trainability Studio; there is no separate Coach account. A forgotten
  password is reset from the sign-in screen, and the temporary one works in
  both apps. Signed in with a temporary password, nothing else opens
  until you choose your own. Each app keeps its own sign-in, so signing in or out of one
  leaves the other as it was.
- **The home screen.** Today's date and a greeting by name on a band shading
  from deep cerulean to sky (a theme's own colors under a theme), your day, and
  cards for Clients, Programs and Messages, with how many clients you have
  and how many messages are waiting.
- **Messages.** Write to a client from the Messages tab and attach pictures,
  videos (up to 100 MB), their programs and their measurements; a program or
  a set of measurements goes as a copy of it as it was, which opens as a page
  ready to print. The client is emailed
  that there is a message (not what it says, and once for a run of them) and
  reads and answers it on their page in Trainability. A red number on the
  Messages button counts the replies you haven't read. Messages from Coach
  are kept in Coach.
  A client's own program, measurements, tests and photos can only be sent
  to that client.
- **Today, on Home.** Your own sessions from Trainability's schedule, today
  and over the next 7 days, laid out as Studio's Today: the time and length,
  the client, where, and First session, New client or Nothing booked after
  this. Each also shows the client's program and how many limitations are
  written for them; tap one to open their program (or their details, when
  they have no program yet). Nothing is booked or changed from Coach.
- **Your clients.** The Clients tab shows everyone on your own list in
  Studio, read from Studio each time so it is never out of date: each one's
  last session, and tapped open, their email, phone, birthday, the day they
  became your client and their next booked session. Search by name, email or
  phone, and archived clients kept under their own tab. Clients are still
  added and changed in Studio.
- **Studio's header and tab bar.** The Coach logo and name across the top,
  and a floating bar at the bottom for Home, Clients, Programs and Messages.
- **Switch between Coach and Studio.** Tap the Coach logo and a panel folds
  down with both apps; Studio is one tap away.
- **Coach IQ's principles in the app.** How Trainability designs a program,
  topic by topic, read, edited, approved and flagged on Review Coach IQ like
  the exercises, with every change kept to undo.
- **Billy works from the principles.** When he talks about reps, sets, load,
  tempo, rest or planning, he reads the approved topics first.
- **Read a book into Coach IQ.** On Review Coach IQ → Books, the Primary
  Master uploads a PDF; Coach reads it in the background and proposes what it
  adds to the principles, topic by topic, asking about anything it shouldn't
  decide alone with a recommended answer. Nothing changes until it is
  accepted.
- **Sign in the way Studio does: your email first.** Type your email and tap
  Continue, then your password. An email that isn't a trainer's is told that
  Coach is for trainers for now, and pointed to its own page in Studio.
- **"You belong here." on the sign-in screen**, in twenty languages under the
  logo, as on Studio's; the Open Studio link is no longer at the foot of it
  (Studio is opened from the apps panel only).
- **Add Coach to your Home Screen.** On a phone, a strip at the bottom of the
  sign-in screen offers it, as in Studio: the steps on an iPhone, the install
  box on Android. From its icon Coach opens full screen.
- **A new app icon, drawn like Studio's.** A white hex on a dark blue tile,
  with the blue C and chevron, for the home screen of your phone and the apps
  panel, named just **Coach** under it. Add Coach to your home screen again to
  pick them up.
- **"What are we working on?"** when adding an exercise: strength, power,
  cardio, balance or mobility and, if you like, muscle groups (chest,
  shoulders, back, arms, core, legs, each opening to its muscles), which narrow Coach IQ's suggestions and go with
  the question when you ask Billy. The next exercise you add to the same
  program opens with the same answers.
- **Coach accounts.** A trainer who isn't on Trainability's team can make a
  Coach account from the sign-in screen (name, email, password) and use
  Coach with it, with clients, programs and everything else, their clients
  their own. A Coach account can be connected to a Trainability account (the
  same email at sign-in, or Link your Trainability account), keeping
  everything; its clients move to Trainability only when you say so. A
  forgotten Coach account password is reset by email with a temporary one,
  as in Studio, and anyone signing in with a temporary password chooses
  their own first.
- **Add, change and archive clients in Coach.** The Clients page has Add a
  client: first and last name, email and phone (birthday, gender and
  pronouns if you like, picked from Studio's lists or typed); someone already a client with that email is put on your list instead
  of being added twice. An open client has Edit details and Archive (or Put
  back on my list). Your clients are the same list as in Studio.
- **Your role badge is in your account menu.** The shield for an Admin,
  Master or Primary Master moved from the top bar to under your name in the
  menu, as in Studio.
- **The new logo.** A bolder chevron and an open C with nothing inside it
  (the dot it had for a while is gone), everywhere: the app icon (flat navy),
  the header, the sign-in screen, the browser tab and the emails; a slightly
  bigger mark in the icon. Remove Coach from your home screen and add it
  again to see the new icon.
- **Measurements.** On a client's details, under Limitations: weight, body
  fat, and neck, shoulders, chest, waist, hips, and arm, thigh and calf left
  and right, with the waist-to-hip ratio worked out, and resting heart rate
  and blood pressure if taken, one set per day, in your own units, with how each changed since the set
  before. Every trainer of the client sees them; only the one who took a set
  can change or delete it. A set can be attached to a message, and opens as a
  page ready to print.
- **Fitness tests.** On a client's details, under Measurements: the
  3-minute step test, rated Excellent to Very poor for their age and for women
  or men; the Rockport 1-mile walk, with their VO2 max worked out; and a VO2
  max from a lab or watch. Age, table and weight are filled in from their
  details and measurements, and each result shows how it changed since the
  same test before. Every trainer of the client sees them; only the one who
  recorded a result can change or delete it. A result can be attached to a
  message, and opens as a page ready to print.
- **Strength tests**, in Fitness tests under their own heading: a 1RM for
  any exercise in the library, lifted or worked out from a lighter set of 10
  reps or fewer, and against body weight; the vertical jump; the drop jump's
  Reactive Strength Index; a wall sit or plank held; and velocity loss from a
  bar-speed sensor. Each shows how it changed since the same test (and
  exercise) before, and can be sent in a message and printed.
- **Progress charts.** Show progress, under a client's measurements, charts
  any measurement taken on two days or more (blood pressure's two numbers
  together); opening a fitness test's earlier results charts that test. Tap a
  point to read its day and number.
- **What a day works.** Each day of a program shows a drawing of a body,
  front and back, its muscles shaded light to dark by how many of the day's
  exercises work them, with the count for each muscle listed beside it. It is on
  the printed program too, and a program sent in a message.
- **Loads from a 1RM.** In a program, write a load as a percentage, such as
  75%, and Coach works it out from the client's latest 1RM for that exercise:
  4 × 5 · 75% (190 lb). Coach IQ's check warns when there is no 1RM to go by,
  when it is over 100%, or when the reps are more than can be done at it.
- **Billy knows the numbers**: a client's measurements, fitness tests and the
  days of their progress photos, with how each changed, and a program's loads
  from a 1RM. He can't see the photos themselves.
- **Wide screen**, Studio's: on a wide computer screen, the button beside the
  panel spreads it across the window. Kept with your account, and shared
  with Studio for a Trainability account.
- **Studio's panel.** On a tablet or a computer, Coach sits in one panel
  with rounded corners on the sign-in screen's blue field, as Studio does,
  the page scrolling inside it. A phone is as before.
- **A client opens on a page of their own**, as large as the screen, headed
  with their name and an arrow back to the list, with four tabs: Details &
  limitations, Measurements, Progress photos and Fitness tests. Each client's
  line on the list says their last session and, when it's time, Measurements
  due.
- **Settings**, in your account menu: your units, and Measurements every,
  how many weeks apart a client's measurements are due (5 to begin with, or
  Off).
- **Progress photos.** On a client's details, under Measurements: the
  front, both sides and the back, once a trainer has recorded that the client
  agrees. Coach guides each photo: a checklist for the setup, then each angle
  in turn with last time's photo faint over the camera to line up with, lines
  for the head and feet, a level on a phone, and a 10-second timer. Compare
  two days side by side or with a slider, and send one day, or two compared,
  in a message. Every trainer of the client sees them; if the client stops
  agreeing, they are all deleted.
- **Photos from Studio.** Your clients' photos, as they are in Studio, on
  the Clients list and in Messages, and your own in the account menu (which
  showed only your initials before). Initials stay for anyone without one.
- **Notifications.** Turn them on for a phone or computer from the account
  menu, as in Studio, and be told when a client writes to you in Messages:
  who, and the start of what they wrote. A tap opens the conversation. Send a
  test shows one arriving. On an iPhone, Coach needs to be on the Home Screen
  first.
- **Accessibility, as in Studio.** In the account menu: light or dark, a
  theme (Default, Purple, Blue, Orange or Green, Coach's own versions),
  contrast, reduced color, motion and text size, with Studio's choices and
  words. A Trainability account's are the same settings as in Studio, so
  changing them in either app changes both; a Coach account keeps its own.
  The look is on before the page draws, and the sign-in screen goes back to
  the device's own when you sign out.
- **Studio's drifting hexagons, in blues.** The logo's hexagon, a dozen
  times over and in four of Coach's blues, drifts slowly behind every page and
  over the sign-in screen, as Studio's purple ones do. Still on a device set to
  reduce motion.
- **Studio's type.** Roboto throughout, headings at medium weight, and
  "Trainability" as a word in Quicksand, as in Studio.
- **Billy's panel joined to his button.** Open, his button and panel are
  one shape, and everything behind them goes grey and blurred; tapping it
  closes him. He and your account menu are never open together.
- **Studio's places are your places.** Every place in Studio shows in My
  equipment under Studio's name, yours (your default place) first, each
  with its own checklist; they follow renames and closures in Studio. A new
  program starts at your place in Studio.
- **A client's gym in Studio is where their program starts.** Their home
  gym, or a membership, is chosen for "Where they train" on a new program,
  read from Studio each time; a program made for another of Studio's places
  says so when opened, and offers the new one.
- **The Primary Master sets the equipment at Studio's places.** Their
  answers show, locked, for every trainer whose place in Studio it is;
  trainers answer only what is left.
- **The client's level comes from their programs.** A new program's level
  is filled in from the ones you made for them before (the next level up
  after 12 weeks at one without stepping back), with the reason shown; with
  none, you're asked. Suggestions put the next step of what they did before
  first, and the check notes a step back.
- **Adding an exercise opens a box of its own.** Pick the exercise (or one
  Coach IQ suggests), see what it works and needs and anything Coach IQ says
  about it for the client, then the sets, reps, load, effort, tempo, rest and
  a note, started from Coach IQ's starting point. Tap an exercise, or
  Details, to change it the same way.
- **A new program starts with what kind it is.** Full body, upper and lower,
  push, pull, legs, a circuit, or mobility; days a week; anything to focus
  on; and how long a session is. The days are made to match, and Coach IQ's
  suggestions keep to what each day trains, with the focus taken in turn.
- **Design programs.** The Programs tab is open: a program for one of your
  clients, day by day, exercise by exercise, with only the exercise needed
  and sets, reps, load, effort, tempo, rest and notes as you like. Coach IQ
  checks it as you go and suggests what could come next, fitted to the
  place's equipment and the client's level and limitations; Swap finds
  stand-ins; Billy can talk it over. Print it for the client.
- **Imperial or metric, your choice.** Under Units in your account menu.
  Imperial to start. Weights and distances in Coach IQ and in Billy's answers
  are shown in your units only, rounded to what a gym has (5 lb or 2.5 kg).
- **Your account menu.** Your photo in the top corner opens a menu with your
  name, email and kind of account, Log out and the version.
- **Billy.** Studio's assistant, beside the badges in the header: ask him
  about the clients on your list, or send him a photo or a PDF — a food log,
  a form, a program — and he reads it. He changes nothing. Shown only where
  the server has a key for him.
- **My equipment.** In your account menu: a short checklist of what you have
  to train with, and the particular pieces a program has asked about. Billy
  checks it before suggesting exercises, asks about a piece once, and notes
  your answer there for you to change.
- **My exercises,** in the account menu: your own names for exercises, tied
  to Coach's library, names still to sort out, and exercises of your own,
  which you can suggest for everybody's library. Billy uses your names, and
  notes one when you tell him.
- **Billy suggests from the library, already checked.** Ask him for
  something instead of an exercise and he offers only what fits the
  equipment at the place, the client's level and their limitations.
- **Review Coach IQ,** for the Primary Master: the library one family at a
  time and the conditions table, each to Approve or Flag with a comment, with
  a count of how far through; trainers' suggestions for the library; and
  Download reviews, to write the marks into the library itself.
- **Installation,** for the Primary Master: **Where files are saved**, as in
  Studio. On the host, files go to its Private Storage (`/private`), which a
  deploy keeps; another folder can be named, and is tried before it is used.
- **Coach IQ's library is edited in the app.** On Review Coach IQ, the
  Primary Master can change, add and retire exercises, add families, change
  and add conditions, and add a trainer's suggestion to the library. A change
  that would break the library is refused with the reason; every change is
  listed under Changes and can be undone; Export the library downloads it
  all.
- **Studio's look throughout.** Roboto, Studio's typeface, with its lighter
  weights and heading sizes, and its kinds of button in Coach's blues: the
  main action a blue pill, other actions outlined, small buttons for one card,
  links with an icon and no underline, Cancel and Keep it in plain grey, and
  deleting a limitation confirmed in a pale red box.
- **Light or dark,** following your phone or computer.
