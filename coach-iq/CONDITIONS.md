# Coach IQ: conditions

Written by `npm run iq` from `conditions.json`. Do not edit this page.

14 conditions (14 drafts for Glenn to approve). For each: the facts and joints
to **avoid** (those exercises are not offered), to **modify** (an easier or supported version is offered first),
whether a doctor's **clearance** comes first, and when to **refer** on. Never a diagnosis.

## Pregnancy, after the first trimester (draft)

- **Avoid:** lying-on-back (lying on the back), abdominal-pressure (strong pressure in the belly), breath-hold (heavy bracing that tempts holding the breath), maximal-effort (all-out effort), inverted (head below the heart). 50 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** high-impact (jumping or running), balance (a balance challenge), lying-face-down (lying face down), spinal-load (a heavy load on the spine). 63 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** yes.
- **Refer:** Bleeding, dizziness, chest pain, calf pain or swelling, fluid loss, or contractions: stop and see their midwife or doctor.

## Postpartum, the first months (draft)

- **Avoid:** abdominal-pressure (strong pressure in the belly), high-impact (jumping or running), maximal-effort (all-out effort). 31 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** breath-hold (heavy bracing that tempts holding the breath), spinal-flexion (rounding the spine), spinal-load (a heavy load on the spine). 14 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** yes.
- **Refer:** Leaking, heaviness or pain in the pelvic floor, or a gap down the middle of the belly: a pelvic health physiotherapist.

## High blood pressure (draft)

- **Avoid:** breath-hold (heavy bracing that tempts holding the breath), maximal-effort (all-out effort), inverted (head below the heart). 19 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** overhead (arms overhead), spinal-load (a heavy load on the spine). 21 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** no.
- **Refer:** If it is not under control, or they feel dizzy, a headache comes on, or their chest hurts: stop and see their doctor.

## A heart condition (draft)

- **Avoid:** maximal-effort (all-out effort), breath-hold (heavy bracing that tempts holding the breath). 14 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** inverted (head below the heart), high-impact (jumping or running). 16 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** yes.
- **Refer:** Chest pain, unusual breathlessness, a racing or irregular heartbeat, or feeling faint: stop at once and get medical help.

## Osteoporosis or low bone density (draft)

- **Avoid:** spinal-flexion (rounding the spine), spinal-rotation (twisting the spine). 15 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** high-impact (jumping or running), spinal-load (a heavy load on the spine), floor-transfer (getting down to the floor and up). 70 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** no.
- **Refer:** A fall, new back pain, or a loss of height: their doctor.

## A knee replacement (draft)

- **Avoid:** kneeling (weight on a knee), high-impact (jumping or running). 27 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** deep-hip-flexion (the hip bent past 90 degrees or the legs crossed), the knees. 68 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** yes.
- **Refer:** Swelling, heat or new pain in the knee: their surgeon or physiotherapist.

## A hip replacement (draft)

- **Avoid:** deep-hip-flexion (the hip bent past 90 degrees or the legs crossed). 29 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** high-impact (jumping or running), kneeling (weight on a knee), balance (a balance challenge), the hips. 69 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** yes.
- **Refer:** Their surgeon's precautions come first; new pain, clicking or a feeling of the hip giving way: their surgeon.

## Vertigo or dizziness (draft)

- **Avoid:** head-movement (quick changes of head position), inverted (head below the heart). 12 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** balance (a balance challenge), floor-transfer (getting down to the floor and up), lying-on-back (lying on the back). 96 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** no.
- **Refer:** New or worsening dizziness, or with a headache, numbness or trouble speaking: medical help at once.

## Poor balance or a risk of falling (draft)

- **Avoid:** high-impact (jumping or running). 14 of 301 exercises left out.
- **Modify:** balance (a balance challenge), floor-transfer (getting down to the floor and up), head-movement (quick changes of head position). 95 more offered with care.
- **Clearance first:** no.
- **Refer:** A recent fall, or falls becoming more frequent: their doctor.
