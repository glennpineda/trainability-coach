# Books read into Coach IQ

For Glenn: which books Coach IQ has learned from, in the order they were read.
Not shown to trainers and not given to Billy. Since build 1.0.16 books are read
in the app, on Review Coach IQ → Books, and listed there; this page keeps the
ones read before.

1. **Program Design: Choosing Reps, Sets, Loads, Tempo and Rest Periods**,
   2nd edition (2011), by Paul Chek (C.H.E.K Institute). Read 17 Oct 2026, in
   two PDFs (pages 1–100, then the course exam, forms and biography). It set
   the outline of `principles/`: terms, designing, reps, sets, load, tempo,
   rest, periodization, frequency and variety, starting out, women and
   teenagers. Written in our own words: none of its text, tables or named
   systems was copied. Glenn settled twelve points where it is dated or one
   coach's view (each recorded in the file it concerns). Its copyright page
   forbids reproducing it and asks that its concepts not be used in paid
   programs without permission; Glenn's decision is to learn from books and
   write Trainability's own (16 Oct 2026).
